Authentic Craftsmanship

From Seed to Bottle: How We Press Our Oil

Our age-old process honors traditional purity without taking shortcuts.

1

Handpicked Seeds

We source only unadulterated, sun-dried groundnuts, unpolished sesame, and sulphur-free copra coconuts.

2

Wooden Ghani Pressing

Seeds are crushed slowly in heavy wooden pestles (Mara Chekku). Friction is kept minimal so temperature never exceeds 40°C.

3

Natural Sedimentation

No chemical bleaching. The freshly pressed oil rests in food-grade vessels under gentle sunlight while tiny seed sediments naturally settle.

4

Fresh Daily Bottling

Pure golden oil is poured directly into food-safe bottles and cans, sealed fresh on the same day for your family.
